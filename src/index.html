About Me

I am a Computer Science graduate from the Technical University of Košice, specializing in Intelligent Systems. During my studies, I worked on individual and team-based projects involving artificial intelligence, backend development, data processing, and web applications.

My strongest practical focus is Python development. I enjoy building useful applications around language models, including RAG systems, document processing pipelines, local GGUF inference, and Gemini API integrations. I also have experience developing Flask and FastAPI backends, designing database-backed workflows, and building Streamlit interfaces for AI experiments.

My academic work includes a bachelor's thesis on prompt engineering for sentiment analysis with large language models and a master's thesis on the robustness of language models to Slovak prompt variations. I am interested in roles where I can continue developing reliable AI-powered software while learning from an experienced engineering team.
